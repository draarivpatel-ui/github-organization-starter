# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this organization's projects to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **conduct@YOUR_DOMAIN**. Maintainers will review reports promptly and may remove content or restrict participation. Reports are kept confidential.
